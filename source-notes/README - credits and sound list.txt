Selected for manga breakdown videos (compact mono MP3s):
- whoosh-soft, whoosh-swipe, whoosh-air : panel cuts / transitions
- impact-sub : hits, action beats
- riser-noise : tension build-up
- bell-ding : reveal / notification accent
- heartbeat : suspense (e.g. before Beru appears)

The repo has 19 sounds in total (WAV, under src/reel_sfx/sounds/<category>/). Re-clone it any time for the rest.
